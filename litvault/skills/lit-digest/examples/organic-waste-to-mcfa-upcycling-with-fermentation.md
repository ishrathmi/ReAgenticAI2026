# Organic waste to MCFA upcycling with fermentation

## Description
Converting organic waste streams (food waste, agro-residues, manure, wastewater, sludge, lignocellulose, syngas/CO2 off-gas) into medium-chain fatty acids (C6-C8: caproate, caprylate, heptanoate) or related chain-elongation products via anaerobic fermentation, open mixed cultures or defined microbes, including product extraction and process engineering.
